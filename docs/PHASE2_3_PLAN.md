# Realty OS: Phase 2 and Phase 3 Plan (authoritative)

Status: **authoritative design for Phases 2 and 3.** It merges two independent drafts, `docs/phase23/draft-a.md` (simplicity-first) and `docs/phase23/draft-b.md` (risk-first), and then applies a second adversarial review by a security critic and a simplicity critic (round 2, verdicts in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §20). For each capability it takes the simpler design unless a draft or a critic showed a concrete hazard; every non-obvious choice is in §2. Scope comes from `docs/VISION.md`. Tables, RLS, rules (`R-*`) and edge cases (E71–E161, and E201–E229 for D2; E162–E193 are FUB parity in Part 3) are in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` Part 2 (§6–§10); the architecture is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §21; the schema is `packages/db/migrations/0018_phase2_3_schema.sql`, proven by `packages/db/test/rls_smoke_p23.sql`. Owner decision **D2** (2026-11-11, §2.2) narrowed and changed the scope (no Meta DMs, Meta Lead Ads or Meta ads; Facebook/Instagram content publishing; Gmail inbox sync added); the D2 migration (`0025_d2_scope_gmail.sql`) adds its schema, proven by `packages/db/test/rls_smoke_gmail.sql` (187 checks).

Phase 1 stays in force and is never weakened: FORCE RLS with composite `(tenant_id, id)` keys, append-only ledgers, ownership only through `applyOwnershipCommand`, every contact communication through `dispatchOutbound`/`dialOutbound` with `canContact` at send time, the assistant acting on its own only inside approved playbooks, money/contracts/new kinds of client message always a tap, an inbound reply pausing automation. **Owner decision D1 stands:** sending checks only opt-outs (STOP and other keywords, unsubscribe, agent-recorded do-not-contact, suppressions), with no override, in core and in the DB guards. Every new channel plugs into that same path and opt-out model. Legal points are marked **VERIFY WITH COUNSEL**, live in data, and ship with the strictest reasonable default; capabilities that wait on counsel or a vendor are gated on a platform clearance pack (R-INT-6), not on a feature flag.
